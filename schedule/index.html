    Course Structure Notes

    • 13 Lectures cover content from 9 weekly learning sheets (Weeks 1-9)
    • Deep learning theory (Weeks 10-12) is not included in the 13-week lecture structure
    • Some weeks are split across multiple lectures for better pacing
    • Each lecture folder (lect1/ - lect13/) contains:
    • Symlinked PDFs to the relevant weekly learning sheets
    • scope.md file defining exact assessment boundaries

    Topic Arcs

    • Lectures 1-5: PAC Learning Theory (Weeks 1-3)
    • Lectures 6-8: Regularization and Model Selection (Weeks 4-5)
    • Lectures 9-10: Boosting Theory (Weeks 6-7)
    • Lectures 11-12: Online Learning - Expert Advice (Week 8)
    • Lecture 13: Online Convex Optimization & Course Synthesis (Week 9)

    Weekly Tests

    Weekly validation tests (30 minutes) will be administered at the beginning of class sessions to assess your understanding of the previous lecture's material.

    • Format: Multiple choice and short answer
    • Duration: 30 minutes
    • Weight: 70% of final grade
    • Sheets available: All weeks (see Validation column above)
